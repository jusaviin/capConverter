NHL Team, Roster & Player Map

See where NHL teams play, where a team's players were born, where a single player was born, or where all the players of one nationality were born. The map automatically updates every time you make a selection. You can also do a quiz trying to guess in which city a player matching your selections was born!

FAQ

Who do you include in a team roster?

A player gets included in a roster if he has played in at least 1 game in the selected season. If a player has played in several teams within a season, he is only included in the roster of the team he ended the season with.

Where do you get player birth city information?

This information is provided by NHL API. The unofficial reference on how to use it can be found from here. If you are using Python, you can use nhl-api-py package to interact with the API.

How do you place cities on the map?

After I get the birth city from NHL API, I use Nominatim from geopy.geocoders to find longitude and latitude for the city. These values are then used to place a blue marker on the map.

How about team logos?

The team logos are placed at the location of their stadium. The list of stadiums can be found on Wikipedia. These stadiums are then geolocated with Nominatim to place the logo in the correct spot.

How does the player tab work?

Type at least two characters of a player's name and press Search (or Enter), then pick the player from the list. The map shows his birth city. Accents are ignored, so "Stutzle" finds Stützle.

How does the nationality tab work?

Choose a season and a nationality to see the birth cities of all players of that nationality who played in the selected season. The nationality list only contains nationalities that were active in that season.

How does the quiz work?

In the quiz, you must find the birth city of the randomly selected player. If you would like, you can restrict the selected players to those on a specific team, those that played during a specific season, or those that played on a specific team during a specific season. Then, select the number of questions and hit "Start quiz." Locate the correct birth city for the player displayed.

Your score for each question depends on how close to the correct answer you were. If you manage to get a perfect score of 0 km away, you are a legend!
